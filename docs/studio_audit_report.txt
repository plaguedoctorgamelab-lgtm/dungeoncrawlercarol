STUDIO POST-BUILD AUDIT: DUNGEON CRAWLER CAROL: LAIR OF THE SEA LORD
Build: commit 78e1a73 (V2 HD overhaul), Godot 4.7.2. Audit date: 2026-11-12.

SCOPE AND METHOD
- I read project.godot, docs/game_design_document.md, docs/master_graphics_asset_manifest.md, all scripts in scripts/ and scenes/, and the generated resources (24 enemies, 4 weapons, 15 projectiles).
- Boot check: "godot --headless --quit" exits 0 with no warnings or parse errors.
- Test suite: scripts/tests/run_unit_tests.gd gives 152 checks and 0 failures.
- I wrote a new probe script, scripts/tools/audit_probe.gd (local only, not on GitHub). It ran across 40 generated rooms and 200 generated maps, and the numbers in this report come from it. It measured:
  - whether the tiles shown as reachable match the real path cost
  - how often ranged shots involve cover
  - fog of war compared with line of sight
  - how long the per-turn refresh takes
  - balance tables for every difficulty and realm
  - overworld pacing
- Note: your brief asks for four department leads but only defines two before it cuts off: the Lead Technical Architect and the Lead Tactical Game Designer. I reviewed those two. Each of them evaluated the game twice: Pass 1 is a read of the code and data, and Pass 2 checks Pass 1 against the probe results.
- The design doc lives at docs/game_design_document.md, not GDD.md in the project root as the brief assumes.

======================================================================
1. LEAD TECHNICAL ARCHITECT
======================================================================

--- PASS 1: CODE AND CONFIG READ ---

WHAT WORKS WELL
- Display config is correct. project.godot sets these:
  - 1920x1080 viewport (lines 22-23)
  - window/size/mode=3, which is windowed fullscreen, not exclusive (line 24)
  - canvas_items stretch with keep aspect (lines 25-26)
  - linear default texture filter (line 42)
  - MSDF fonts (line 30)
- The F11 toggle is in GameState._input (scripts/autoload/GameState.gd:64-76). It calls set_input_as_handled() and the autoload uses PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS, so it works on every screen, including paused ones.
- The isometric grid is set up correctly:
  - IsometricGrid.CELL = Vector2i(128, 64) (scenes/tactical/IsometricGrid.gd:8).
  - Both the floor and fog TileSets use TILE_SHAPE_ISOMETRIC with TILE_LAYOUT_DIAMOND_DOWN (lines 62, 83).
  - All coordinate math goes through ground.map_to_local / local_to_map. There is no hand-rolled isometric math to drift out of sync.
- SpriteScaler does what the spec asks. SpriteScaler.fit (scripts/util/SpriteScaler.gd:22-33):
  - uses one uniform scale, min(footprint.x/w, footprint.y/h), so sprites are never distorted
  - anchors the sprite bottom-center with offset.y = -h/2 + sink/s (line 30)
  - the test suite confirms a raw 512x512 texture lands at exactly 128, 256 or 56 px
  - missing PNGs fall back to procedural placeholders through SpriteScaler.texture()
- Boss footprint is cleanly modelled. BattleUnit.size_cells, footprint(), occupies() and nearest_cell() (scenes/units/BattleUnit.gd:20-45) give 2x2 bosses correct occupancy, targeting, range and line of sight. No special cases are scattered through the battle code.
- The enemy turn loop can't hang:
  - _enemy_act is capped at "guard < 12" (TacticalBattlefield.gd:515)
  - every move or attack consumes AP or breaks out of the loop
  - _boss_step returns false when it's blocked (line 562 onward)
  - every await is on a tween that always finishes
- The save format is defensive. SaveManager.read_slot handles corrupt JSON by returning {} (scripts/autoload/SaveManager.gd:42). GameState._normalize_map turns JSON floats back into ints.

CRITICAL ISSUES AND RISKS
1. Cyan tiles can't always be reached (a correctness bug).
   - IsometricGrid.reachable() (line 218) is an unweighted BFS.
   - add_hazard() gives spike tiles weight 5.0 in the AStarGrid2D (line 175).
   - player_move() uses that weighted A* path and rejects any path longer than Carol's AP (TacticalBattlefield.gd:406).
   - Result: a tile can be highlighted as reachable, but clicking it silently does nothing because A* detours around the spikes.
2. Shots go through vending machines next to the shooter.
   - has_los() exempts every line-of-sight body next to EITHER end of the shot (IsometricGrid.gd:244-247).
   - If Carol stands directly behind a vending machine on the firing line, she shoots straight through it, and so do enemies.
   - The exemption is meant to let a unit "peek" out of its own cover, but it isn't limited to tiles beside the firing line.
3. Fog of war ignores line of sight.
   - update_fog() reveals every tile within a radius (IsometricGrid.gd:276), so Carol sees through vending machines.
   - Enemy detection (TacticalBattlefield.gd:507) is also distance-only.
4. Permadeath has two loopholes. _defeat() wipes only GameState.active_slot (TacticalBattlefield.gd:688).
   - Loophole A: an EXTREME run saved to another slot survives the wipe. SaveMenu lets you save to any of the 3 slots at a level start.
   - Loophole B: EXTREME only auto-saves at level start. A player who quits the game before the killing blow and reloads skips permadeath entirely.
5. Save writes are not atomic. save_game() opens the live file for WRITE and streams into it (SaveManager.gd:27-37). A crash or power loss mid-write leaves a truncated file. read_slot() treats that as an empty slot, which on EXTREME is indistinguishable from a wipe.
6. Loaded saves aren't validated. from_dict() restores equipped_weapon from JSON with no check that the weapon still exists (GameState.gd:250).
   - A save edited by hand, or written by a future build with renamed weapons, gives weapon_data() == null.
   - The first current_weapon().ap_cost read then crashes the battle.
   - The save "version" field is still 2 after the V2 schema change.

ARCHITECTURE NOTES (lower severity)
- "Signals up, methods down" is mostly followed:
  - The battle emits through SignalBus (hp_changed, ap_changed, hit_preview, phase_changed).
  - The HUD calls battle methods directly.
  - But TacticalHUD holds "var battle: Node2D" (scenes/ui/TacticalHUD.gd:4) and calls battle.use_drink(), battle.enemies and so on untyped. TacticalBattlefield has no class_name, so none of that is checked by the type system.
- The autoloads depend on each other in a cycle:
  - GameState.autosave() reaches SaveManager through get_node_or_null("/root/SaveManager") (GameState.gd:151).
  - SaveManager calls GameState.to_dict().
  - It works because both are autoloads, but it's hidden coupling.
- Two scene nodes don't do what their names suggest:
  - y_sort_enabled on the TacticalBattlefield root (TacticalBattlefield.tscn:9) has no effect. Its children are a CanvasLayer, Grid, Camera2D and the HUD. The sorting that matters is on Grid/Objects (line 39), where units, props and projectiles live.
  - Grid/Effects (line 41, IsometricGrid.gd:20) is now unused because projectiles moved to Objects.
- 2x2 boss depth-sort ties: a boss's position is the centre of its 2x2 block, so a unit on certain diagonal tiles (for example anchor+(2,-1)) has exactly the same y and draws in tree order.
- UiKit.fit_design (scripts/ui/UiKit.gd:70-78) scales the 1280x720 UI layouts by a factor from ProjectSettings, not from the live viewport. That's fine under "keep" stretch, but it will break if anyone changes the stretch mode.

SPECIFIC RECOMMENDATIONS
- Make reachable() use the same costs as the A*. Either run Dijkstra with the hazard weight, or keep the BFS and stop weighting spikes in the A*. Then add a test asserting that every cyan tile's path cost is within Carol's AP.
- In has_los(), only exempt cover next to the shooter if that cover isn't on the direct line, or pick an explicit "step-out" tile beside it the way XCOM does.
- Give fog of war and enemy detection the same RayCast2D line-of-sight check that shooting uses. update_fog() can call has_los() for every tile in the radius at almost no cost (see Pass 2 timings).
- Fix permadeath:
  - write the EXTREME save after every completed node, so quitting doesn't help
  - tag saves with the run_seed and wipe every slot that holds the same run on death
  - write saves to a temp file, then rename it into place (DirAccess.rename)
- Validate weapons and equipped_weapon against Roster in from_dict(), and bump the save version to 3.
- Add class_name TacticalBattlefield and type TacticalHUD.battle. Delete the Grid/Effects node, and either drop the root y_sort flag or document it as cosmetic.

--- PASS 2: RUNTIME VERIFICATION (audit_probe.gd + test runner) ---

What the probes confirmed:
- Issue 1, unreachable cyan tiles: confirmed.
  - Across 40 rooms there were 1,083 reachable tiles, and 8 of them (0.7%) had a weighted A* path longer than Carol's AP.
  - Clicking those 8 silently fails.
  - That's rare, but it happens most often right next to spike traps, which is exactly where a player is watching closely.
  - I'm raising it from "edge case" to "ship blocker for feel".
- Issue 3, fog: confirmed. Of 2,830 tiles shown as visible, 285 (10.1%) are not in line of sight from Carol. One in ten "visible" tiles is behind solid cover.
- Performance is not a risk. The per-action refresh is cheap, so adding line-of-sight to the fog is affordable:

  | Call | Time |
  | --- | --- |
  | attack_range_cells() (a raycast per tile, 144 tiles) | 0.20 ms |
  | full _refresh_player_view() | 0.35 ms |
  | update_fog() | 0.10 ms |

- Boot and stability: the headless boot is clean. 152 of 152 checks pass, including the 4-boss gauntlet, Penny's rescue, save/load and the permadeath wipe. Across all 40 probe rooms there were no SCRIPT ERRORs and no stuck enemy phases.

Revised priority order:
1. Unreachable cyan tiles (player-facing, silent)
2. Permadeath loopholes (they undermine the EXTREME difficulty)
3. Shooting through vending machines
4. Fog ignoring line of sight
5. Atomic saves and validating loaded saves
6. Typing and tidying the scene

Pass 1 overweighted the architecture notes. Measured at runtime, the codebase is stable and fast, and the real risks are rules that are mathematically inconsistent, not crashes.

======================================================================
2. LEAD TACTICAL GAME DESIGNER
======================================================================

--- PASS 1: RULES AND DATA READ ---

WHAT WORKS WELL
- The 4 AP economy is easy to read: moving costs 1 AP per tile and every attack costs 2 AP (Roster.WEAPONS). Each turn reduces to "move 2 and attack, attack twice, or move 4", which keeps the 1990s arcade pace. Nothing costs 3 AP, so AP never strands awkwardly.
- Melee is NOT the weaker choice, because melee ignores cover (TacticalBattlefield.attack_cover). Melee enemies also walk into Carol's reach.
  - The Squeaky Foam Mallet (36 dmg) has the best expected damage in the game.
  - The Neon Star-Sword stays relevant against targets in cover.
- Enemy roles differ in ways that matter in play:
  - fast units (tiles_per_ap 2): mower, bat, flame imp, top-hat bug
  - armoured tanks (damage_reduction 0.1-0.3)
  - ranged units with distinct projectiles
  - each monster attacks at most once per turn, so a 4-unit squad stays survivable
- The node map is structured well: 15-20 nodes, the boss in the last 25% by node number, every path reaches the boss (tested), and there are three non-combat node types for pacing.
- The Super Soaker 9000 is guaranteed when the Fire realm starts (GameState.start_realm, line 101). It can also turn up earlier in caches, since it's in Roster.DROP_WEAPONS, which matches "before/during Fire".

CRITICAL ISSUES AND RISKS
1. Hit chance ignores distance.
   - CombatMath.hit_chance only looks at cover, so the Nailgun hits 85% at 7 tiles, the same as point blank.
   - With no distance penalty, there's no reason to close in with a ranged weapon.
   - Hanging back is always right, which works against the arcade energy.
2. The Contractor Nailgun is a trap pick.
   - Expected damage per AP (from the probe):

     | Weapon | In the open | Half cover | Full cover |
     | --- | --- | --- | --- |
     | Nailgun | 6.8 | 4.8 | 2.8 |
     | Sword | 10.6 | 10.6 (ignores cover) | 10.6 |
     | Soaker | 14.5 | 10.2 | 6.0 |
     | Mallet | 15.3 | 15.3 (ignores cover) | 15.3 |

   - Once the Soaker drops, the Nailgun's only advantage is 2 extra tiles of range.
3. Enemies never use cover.
   - _enemy_step paths straight at carol.cell (TacticalBattlefield.gd:540).
   - Ranged enemies walk forward until they're in range with line of sight. They never look for tiles that give them cover, never flank to strip Carol's cover, and never retreat.
   - The cover system is one-sided: only the player uses it, and only when they choose to.
4. Fog on EXTREME breaks ranged play.
   - EXTREME sight is 3 tiles (GameState.gd:12), but enemy attack ranges are 4-6 tiles.
   - Enemies shoot from tiles Carol can't see, and are only revealed after firing.
   - The Nailgun's 7-tile range is useless unless a target has just revealed itself.
5. Bosses scale harder than Carol does.
   - Boss HP and damage scale with difficulty, while Carol's damage only grows +20% per realm (GameState.carol_damage_mult) and her HP stays at 100.
   - On EXTREME the Sea Lord deals 64 per hit, so 2 hits kill Carol.
6. The map often looks like it loops back.
   - The boss is any node in the final 25% by node number, not necessarily in the final layer.
   - When it isn't, nodes in later layers link backwards to it (OverworldGenerator.gd:53-61). On the map that looks like the path loops back.

SPECIFIC RECOMMENDATIONS
- Add a gentle distance falloff on top of cover, for example -2% per tile beyond 4, so close-range aggression is rewarded and long Nailgun shots are a real trade-off.
- Rework the Nailgun: either 1 AP per shot (twice the actions, keeping the current damage) or a 20% hit bonus that ignores half cover. Either gives it a role instead of being the Soaker's weaker twin.
- Add one enemy AI rule: when a ranged enemy can attack from several tiles, prefer the one where cover_between(carol, tile) is highest. It's about 15 lines and makes the cover system work in both directions.
- On EXTREME, raise sight to 4 and cap enemy ranged attacks at sight+1, or reveal an enemy for one turn when it attacks out of fog, so a hit from the dark always gives the player a target.
- Scale Carol with difficulty too: +10 max HP per realm cleared, or more Energy Drinks on Hard and EXTREME, instead of only enemy multipliers.
- Always put the boss in the final layer, and use "final 25%" to choose which lane it's in.

--- PASS 2: NUMBERS FROM THE PROBE (40 rooms, 200 maps, all difficulty and realm combinations) ---

Cover in practice:
- Of 5,431 possible ranged shots within 7 tiles, 81.3% were in the open, 7.9% against half cover, 1.8% against full cover, and 9.0% had no line of sight.
- The average hit chance, when a shot was possible, was 81.8%.
- Verdict: cover hardly ever triggers. It doesn't slow the game or cause frustrating misses. It's barely in play at all.
- Cover objects are spread at random rather than placed next to likely firing positions, and the enemy AI doesn't seek them (Pass 1, issue 3).
- I'm upgrading the recommendation: as well as cover-seeking AI, _place_terrain (TacticalBattlefield.gd:161) should cluster cover into deliberate "fighting positions". Right now 12-17 props are scattered at random (line 165), with 40% of them vending machines (line 173).

Boss fights, as hits to kill Carol and shots for Carol to kill the boss. The shot counts are Carol's best ranged weapon at 85% (open) and 35% (target in full cover):

| Fight | Hits to kill Carol | Carol's shots (open) | Carol's shots (full cover) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medium, realm 1 (Golem, 160 HP, 24 dmg) | 5 | 11.8 (Nailgun) | 28.6 |
| Medium, realm 4 (Sea Lord, 300 HP, 40 dmg) | 3 | 6.5 (Soaker) | 15.8 |
| Hard, realm 4 (390 HP, 52 dmg) | 2 | 8.4 | 20.5 |
| EXTREME, realm 4 (480 HP, 64 dmg) | 2 | 10.4 | 25.2 |

- On EXTREME, Carol needs at least 5 turns of double shots against a boss that kills her in 2 hits. Survival depends entirely on her own cover, which the probe shows is rarely available.
- EXTREME's realm 4 is very probably unwinnable without luck. Hard is tight but fair.
- Easy and Medium are comfortable.

The realm 1 boss is a slog:
- With only the Nailgun, the Golem takes 11.8 shots (6 turns) on Medium and 18.8 on EXTREME. That's slower than the realm 4 boss with the Soaker (6.5).
- The difficulty curve is inverted: the first boss feels slower than the last.
- This confirms the Nailgun issue and strengthens the case for making sure the Mallet or Soaker is available before the Golem.

Ranged enemies firing from beyond sight:
- Easy, Medium and Hard: 0 of 2-3 ranged enemies per realm can outrange Carol's sight.
- EXTREME: 2 of 2, 3 of 3, 2 of 2 and 1 of 2 across the four realms.
- Pass 1 issue 4 is confirmed. On EXTREME almost every ranged enemy can shoot from fog.

Melee enemies, as sword hits to kill an average one:
- 1.1-1.4 hits on Easy and Medium, 1.8-2.3 on Hard, 2.2-2.8 on EXTREME.
- On Hard and EXTREME a melee squad needs two sword swings each, so Carol must stand still to double-swing. 4 AP feels tight there, but it isn't starved.

Overworld pacing, across 200 maps:
- An average map has 17.5 nodes in 7.3 layers.
- A path to the boss passes through 6.7-8.2 nodes, and about 57% of non-boss nodes are combat (2,010 combat, 623 hazard, 671 supply). That's roughly 4-5 fights plus the boss per realm, which is good roguelite pacing.
- In 111 of 200 maps (55%) the boss is not in the last layer, so the "loops back" problem from Pass 1 is the norm, not an edge case. I'm raising it to high priority, because it makes the map hard to read.

Resource attrition: Carol recovers 15 HP after each won fight and fully heals each realm, and drinks come from 35% of hazards, 30% of non-boss kills, and any cache that doesn't give a weapon. On Easy and Medium that's plenty. On Hard and EXTREME, the boss maths above shows the problem is burst damage, not attrition, so more drinks alone won't fix it.

Revised priority order:
1. Enemies seeking cover, plus deliberate placement of cover (cover is barely in play right now)
2. Always put the boss in the final layer
3. Fix EXTREME: sight vs range, and boss burst damage
4. Rework the Nailgun and fix the slow first boss
5. Add a distance falloff to hit chance

CROSS-DEPARTMENT TOP 8 (in priority order)
