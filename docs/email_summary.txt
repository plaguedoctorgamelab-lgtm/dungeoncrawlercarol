Dungeon Crawler Carol: Lair of The Sea Lord is built in Godot 4.7 from the V2 design document and the sprite pack, and it's uploaded to GitHub.

Repo: https://github.com/plaguedoctorgamelab-lgtm/dungeoncrawlercarol
Windows build: releases/DungeonCrawlerCarol_V2_Windows.zip in the repo (unzip it and run DungeonCrawlerCarol_V2.exe)

WHAT WAS BUILT
- Title screen with 4 difficulties (Easy, Medium, Hard, EXTREME!!!!!!) and 3 save slots.
- Overworld: a procedural branching node map per realm with 15-20 nodes. Node types are Fight, Hazard, Supply Cache and Boss. The boss always spawns in the last 25% of nodes, and every path leads to it.
- Tactical battles on an isometric 32x16 TileMapLayer grid, with AStarGrid2D pathfinding and 4 AP per turn (1 AP per tile moved, 2-3 AP per attack, 1 AP for an energy drink). Turns go Player Phase, then Enemy AI Phase.
- Hit chance = max(30%, base - distance x 5%), with a melee base of 95% and a ranged base of 85%. Line of sight is checked with a RayCast2D, and vending machines block shots (0% chance).
- Fog of war. Your sight radius depends on difficulty (8/6/5/4 tiles), and enemies stay hidden until you can see them.
- Projectiles are static sprites that fly at 400 px/s. All 15 projectile sprites are used.
- All 24 monsters and 4 bosses with the stats from the design document, scaled by difficulty. Weapons are the Neon Star-Sword, Super Soaker 9000, Squeaky Mallet and Contractor Nailgun.
- Beating the Sea Lord breaks Princess Penny's cage and plays the "Rescue Complete" victory screen.
- JSON saves (user://savegame.json). Easy and Medium can save at every node, Hard only at the start of each realm. On EXTREME, dying wipes the save file and restarts at Level 1.

WHAT HAPPENED
- The headless test command from the design document (godot --headless --script res://scripts/tests/run_unit_tests.gd) passes all 98 checks. It boots to the title screen, generates 100 overworld maps, plays a mock tactical turn, fights all 4 bosses including the Penny rescue, and checks saving, loading and the permadeath wipe.
- The headless Windows export works. The .exe launches without errors.
- I made real screenshots of every screen and fixed two layout bugs they showed. The screenshots are in docs/screenshots.
- To build the .exe I downloaded the official Godot 4.7.2 export templates, with your OK.

CHANGES FROM THE DESIGN DOCUMENT, FOR BALANCE
- Each monster attacks at most once per turn.
- Carol does +20% damage per realm cleared. She heals 15 HP after each fight and fully between realms.
- Fast monsters move 2 tiles per AP. Armored tanks ignore part of incoming damage.
- The hit-chance badge art has "85%" baked into it, so the HUD shows the live number as text instead.
